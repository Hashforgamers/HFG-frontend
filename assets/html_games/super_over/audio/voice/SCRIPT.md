# Hash Super Over — commentary recording script

Record each line as its own clip (1–3 s, energetic broadcast tone), name it
`<moment>_<n>.mp3`, drop it in this folder and list it under its moment in
`index.json`. The game picks a random clip per moment; any moment left out
just stays silent (the on-screen ticker still runs).

Example `index.json` entry: `"bowled": ["bowled_1.mp3", "bowled_2.mp3", "bowled_3.mp3"]`

Delivery tips: mono, 44.1 kHz, ~-16 LUFS, trim silence at both ends, no music bed.

## Boundaries
**six**
1. That's huge! Into the crowd!
2. Gone all the way! Maximum!
3. Out of the stadium! What a hit!
4. Six! Clean as you like!

**four**
1. Cracking shot! Four!
2. Timed to perfection, races away!
3. No stopping that one! Four runs!
4. Threaded the gap beautifully!

## Wickets
**bowled**
1. Timber! The stumps are a mess!
2. Through the gate! Bowled him!
3. Castled! What a delivery!

**caught**
1. In the air… and taken!
2. Straight down his throat! Caught!
3. Safe hands! He has to go!

**behind** (caught by the keeper)
1. Edged… and taken behind!
2. Just a feather, the keeper does the rest!
3. Nicked off! Gone!

**runout**
1. Run out! Never a run there!
2. Brilliant fielding, he's short of his ground!
3. Mix-up in the middle, and he's gone!

**stumped** *(not voiced yet — record it and I'll wire it in)*
1. Down the track, beaten, stumped!
2. Lightning glovework! Stumped!

## Near misses & extras
**beaten** (swing and a miss)
1. Beaten all ends up!
2. Oh, that's a beauty! Past the edge!
3. Swing and a miss!

**drop** (dropped catch)
1. Oh, he's put it down!
2. Dropped! That could be costly!
3. A life for the batter!

**notout** (bowled on a no-ball or free hit)
1. Knocked him over… but he survives!
2. Stumps flying, but it's not out!

**noball**
1. No ball! He's overstepped!
2. That's a no ball, free hit coming!

**freehit**
1. Free hit! Swing for the stands!
2. Free hit, nothing to lose here!

## Pressure moments
**lastover**
1. Last over! This is it!
2. Six balls left, everything to play for!

**lastball**
1. Last ball! It all comes down to this!
2. One ball left… hold your breath!

## Results
**win**
1. They've done it! What a finish!
2. Chased it down! Victory!
3. Get in! That's the match!

**flawless** (won without losing a wicket)
1. Flawless! Not a wicket lost!
2. Total domination! Perfect chase!

**lose**
1. So close, but not today!
2. Fell just short! Heartbreak!
3. The bowlers win this one!

**tie**
1. It's a tie! Unbelievable!
2. Scores level! You couldn't script it!

**newbest** (new personal best)
1. New personal best! Take a bow!
2. That's a new record!
